Privacy Policy — Pro Beach Volley

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what data Pro Beach Volley (“the game”), published by Yellow Crystal Games (“we”, “us”), collects, why, who processes it, and how you can have it deleted.

Data controller: Kristina Nikonenok, trading as Yellow Crystal Games.
Contact: privacy@yellowcrystalgames.com

1. What we collect and why

DataWhenWhy
Your Meta user ID and a one-time sign-in proofAt launchTo check that you own the game and to sign you in to our online services

We do not collect your real name, email address, payment details, precise location, camera data, or anything from your Meta profile beyond your username and your friends list. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your game progress (career, cosmetics, settings) is stored on your headset. If your headset's cloud backup is on, the save file is backed up by your device's backup service; we never see it.

2. Who processes the data

  • Meta Platforms — sign-in, ownership check, usernames, block list, invites and achievements and your friends list. See Meta's privacy policy.
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Lobby, Relay, Vivox voice chat, Leaderboards, Cloud Save, Diagnostics, Analytics) — processes the data above on our behalf to run online play, voice chat, leaderboards, crash reporting and analytics. Data may be processed in the United States and in other countries where Unity operates. See Unity's privacy policy.

3. How long we keep it

  • Online sign-in records: while you play the game; deleted on request.
  • Leaderboard scores and your public Meta user ID: while the game's online services run; deleted on request.
  • Voice, avatar movement and room data: not kept after the room closes.
  • Crash reports and analytics: no longer than 24 months, then deleted or anonymised.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Analytics is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Voice chat: turn your microphone off with the Mic button on the Online tab, or mute any other player.
  • Block any player through Meta's block dialog from the room's player list.
  • Access and deletion: email privacy@yellowcrystalgames.com with your Meta username, and within 30 days we will delete the data our online services hold about you (including your leaderboard scores), or send you a copy of it. Uninstalling the game deletes the save on your headset.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may also have the right to correct or restrict the processing of your data, to object to it, and to complain to your data-protection authority.

5. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

6. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
