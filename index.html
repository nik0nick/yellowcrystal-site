Beach volleyball, played with your own arms.

Serve, dig, set, spike and block for real, then take your partner from small Challenger beaches all the way to the World Tour Finals.

Every touch is yours

No buttons for shots. The game reads your arms and works out the ball's flight, so a pass is a pass and a spike is a swing.

  1. Serve

    Toss it up and swing. How hard you hit is how deep it goes.

  2. Pass

    Arms together, angle the platform. Don't swing, let the ball do the work.

  3. Set

    Two hands over your forehead, push it high for your hitter.

  4. Spike

    Jump, swing hard for a kill, or roll a soft one over the block.

  5. Block

    Call line or angle behind your back, then jump at the net and stuff it.

Go on tour

A career with Leo, a partner who gets better as you do. Coach Mako reads your game, Dee sets the month's goals and signs you to sponsors, and four rival pairs remember every match you've played them.
